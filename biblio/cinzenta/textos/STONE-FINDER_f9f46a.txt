Easy search tool for architects, designers, and sculptors


The STONE FINDER by Stone-Ideas.com is a database where architects, designers, and sculptors
can easily search for the natural stone of their choice.

The main features are:

* it is free, both for companies presenting their stones and for users. No registration is needed;

* only quarriers may show their stones in the database. The reason is: the stone sector is
characterized by manufactures where - from quarrying to processing (and sometimes even
installing) - all the steps are in one hand. This results in special confidence between the buyer
and the seller – we want to strengthen this culture as it helps to compete with industries like
artificial stone or ceramics.

The STONE FINDER web page. More details under our Questions & Answers.

We launched the STONE FINDER last summer and it is rapidly growing.

Until now, we mainly have usual marbles, granites, limestones, sandstones etc. in our database.
For the future, we’d be glad to also have exotic stones, so Brazilian granites and quartzites
famous for extraordinary colors and structures.
If that sounds interesting for you please send a Mail to Peter (BECKER, editor-in-chief and
publisher of Stone-Ideas.com). We will send you the form where you can fill in the needed
information. Please find below a photo of the form.

Please note: As our target group is architects, designers, and artists, it is best to show the stone
how it is used at facades, in kitchens, bathrooms, or gardens. Creative people want inspirations.
Yet the main picture of a presentation must be a close-up photo, of course. See examples on the
web page.

As Stone-Ideas.com has been reporting about architecture, design, and art topics for 15 years,
we have strong ties to these target groups to promote the STONE FINDER. Once we have
reached a „critical mass“ of entries, we will start the international promotion.
